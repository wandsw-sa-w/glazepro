## 1. The mid rails, and which number is stored

The brief asked for the **muntin** centred; the generator centred the **mid
rails** too. A front door's lock rail sits LOW — long panels or glass above, a
short panel below:

| Template | Integrate | as run | now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front Door (Balham) | 983 / 635, rail 170 | 809 / 809 | **983 / 635** |
| Front Door With Fanlight | glass 941 / panel 618, rail 170 | 780 / 779 | **941 / 618** |
| Arched Front Door | glass 1103 / panel 582, rail 120 | 843 / 842 | **1103 / 582** |

**The number stored is 983 / 941 / 1103 — the height of the cells ABOVE the
rail.** That is the board's convention for every divider, in `spans()`: a
divider's `offset` is the span BEFORE it, and its thickness comes out of what
is left. So the bottom cell is never stored at all; `leafCells` works it out
as `interior − offset − thickness`, which is why the three add up:

```
Balham   1788 − 983 − 170 = 635
Bedford  1729 − 941 − 170 = 618
Arched   1805 − 1103 − 120 = 582
```

A test asserts that sum for every template, and that the stored offset IS the
top cell — so the convention is pinned, not remembered.

`setLeafGrid` now takes `rowOffsets` / `columnOffsets`; anything not given is
still spaced evenly, which is what the layout buttons on the board want.
`FRONT_DOOR_TEMPLATES` carries Integrate's three as `rowOffsets`. **The muntin
stays centred at 329 / 328** — Integrate's 373 / 284 is its own template's
accident, as the brief said.
